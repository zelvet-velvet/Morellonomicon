## 🧮 Augment model: tiers from the champion's own numbers (Claude, for Zaviel)
- **New design note [[Augment Model]]**: each augment is ranked for each champion play style by how much stronger it makes that play style in a typical fight (damage, survival, support, mobility), worked out from the champion's own numbers; then compared within the champion and across champions to give S / A / B / C with a reason.
- **New script `tools/augment_model.py`** and hand files for augment terms and champion corrections (Katarina's cooldown resets, a second 攻速特效 play style). The first 32 augments are modelled.
- **Your first review applied**: casting now costs an auto-attacker attack time; abilities that apply on-hit or empower an attack count as attacks (祕術拳擊 now S for Fiora and Ezreal, B for Jinx); support counts only for 輔助 champions; Master Yi's double strike, E, R and Q cooldown are in. On-hit marked on Ezreal, Fiora and Master Yi's Q.
- **Second review applied**: attacks that shorten cooldowns feed back (Master Yi's Q spam with 祕術拳擊, untargetable time as survival); Yasuo and Yone's Q (on-hit, crits, faster with attack speed); Jinx's R damage; a hit chance for late damage (大地覺醒 rarely hits); champions compared by absolute gain, and S needs both a top augment for the champion and a top user of the augment.
- **Third review applied**: an attack-empowering ability no longer counts as an extra attack (only attack resets do), tanks attack less, Jinx's W winds up longer, AP Malphite as its own play style; tiers now compare each augment with the champion's best augment and with the augment's best users (Jinx's 祕術拳擊 B, Malphite's haste above 祕術拳擊).
- **Picks and rarity**: the fight is worked out at levels 3, 7, 11, 15 and 18 (ranks and items for each), an augment valued by when it can be picked (3 / 7 / 11 / 15) and marked 前期強 / 後期強; tiers are per rarity (a screen's three offers share one); the Pentakill band's 金屬 set ranked apart (末日金屬 only at 18). Katarina's R counts once for 祕術拳擊 and is cut by crowd control.
- **All 248 augments modelled**: 204 as terms, 44 left unrated with a reason (quests, random ones, item upgrades, snowball and summoner-spell swaps, map-only and disabled). Augments for one kind of ability (spinning, terrain, channelling, missiles, knock-backs, crowd control, shields, ally healing) count only for champions that have one; once-a-fight effects with long cooldowns count in part of the fights; about the top two or three of a rarity are S.
- **Tier page proposed in [[Layouts]]** (Champions · 強化 tier options): A one rarity at a time with tabs, B every rarity's S and A on one page. Not built until one is picked.
- **For review: [[Augment Tiers]]** lists the tiers of thirteen test champions (Fiora, Yasuo, Yone added) (Katarina, Jinx, Ezreal, Malphite, Soraka, Darius, Master Yi, Lux, Kai'Sa, Garen). Not in the app yet.
