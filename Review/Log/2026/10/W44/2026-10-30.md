## 📖 Wiki numbers for the Mayhem augments (Claude, for Zaviel)
- 145 augments that the game data has no numbers for now have a zh_TW effect line with the numbers from the League of Legends Wiki, e.g. 三度傷害: every third attack deals 6% (ranged 3%) of the target's max health as true damage. Riot's own wording where it had one, translated where not; marked 待驗證.
- The augment page credits the wiki (CC BY-SA) and the patch under the text; the README lists the wiki as a source. `tools/fetch_wiki_augments.py` keeps the wiki's text and lists the augments it changed at each patch.
- Found: Arena's numbers differ from the wiki for 34 of 54 augments (蠻力重擊 10% vs 20%), so the page now says those numbers are Arena's.

## 🔮 New augment page with real numbers (Claude, for Zaviel)
- **Augment page A** (chosen by Zaviel): a banner like the item page, a Lv1 / 最大 switch for the 47 augments that upgrade, stat chips with the shop icons (暴擊率, 攻速, 物攻…), the effect with its numbers filled in and coloured by kind (magic blue, physical orange, heal green…), the fitting champions as a strip that scrolls sideways, and the history in one line.
- **Numbers from the game data**: 102 of the 249 augments, those shared with Arena (e.g. 鈍器重擊 10% → 25% 物攻). The augment list shows these numbers too. The 147 Mayhem-only augments keep their text without numbers.
- **Who it suits**: for 29 augments Riot's own tags say which champions can be offered them (e.g. a mana augment is never offered to champions without mana); the others keep the guess from the text.
