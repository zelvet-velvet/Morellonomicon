## 🧭 Scope: Summoner's Rift and Mayhem (Claude, for Zaviel)
- The project is described as a guide for both Summoner's Rift and ARAM Mayhem (CLAUDE.md, README, Home, LLM Guide, the plugin's description), not Mayhem only; Mayhem was built first and Rift is being filled in.

## 🗺️ Handbook: loading screen, picture tiles, text panel cards (Claude, for Zaviel)
- Picked from mockups (A top + B list + Card 2): the Handbook opens with the loading tip on an ARAM map, then the topics as pictures (the next one large with 繼續, the rest two across, ✓ when read) and the glossary row. Each topic's picture is its new `cover:` (a champion splash or an ARAM map).
- Topic cards: the picture on the top half, then a text panel: "1 / 6", the title, the line, and the 大混戰裡 tip in its own box. Chinese text breaks only between phrases and never starts a line with ，。; the guide text now uses full-width punctuation for that.

## 🃏 Handbook cards fixed (Claude, for Zaviel)
- Skill clips are no longer cropped: the whole clip, over a blurred copy of itself that fills the card. Ability tags wrap inside the card at a larger size (they ran off the screen). Cards with only an icon show it plain and large, without the big frame.
- Role cards show six iconic champions each (蓋倫 達瑞斯 賈克斯 …, 墨菲特 鄂爾 …) instead of a random daily six; 四個分頁 shows the champion page's tabs, 三種傷害 the three damage colours, 一條路打到底 the Howling Abyss loading screen.
- ARAM item copies take the normal item's current picture whenever Riot's data has that item, even when the normal item is not in our list (深淵權杖 773001 → 3001); next patch run.

## 📘 Handbook redesign, round two (Claude, for Zaviel)
- Home is one calm list: a thin progress line, a row per topic (number or ✓, icon, serif title, one line) with 繼續 on the next one, the glossary as the last row and the loading tip at the bottom; the orange hero, the tiles and the "1 分鐘" labels are gone; glossary terms scroll in one row.
- Card pictures fill the card: clips and screenshots edge to edge, icons, charts, role / lane icons and item / champion strips scaled up (strips 3 across at 84 px).
- No separate end card: pulling the last card further left makes it shiver with a haptic tick, then it follows the finger at a fraction of the distance and shows the next topic behind it; past the mark it turns orange (another tick) and letting go opens that topic, otherwise the card springs back. After the last topic it leads back to the Handbook.

## 💡 Loading screen tips in the Handbook (Claude, for Zaviel)
- The tip card's background is one of ARAM's three loading screen backgrounds, at random per visit (game/assets/ux/loadingscreen/habackground, arambilgewater, arambloom on CommunityDragon, found with the new `tools/find_loading_art.py` → `Review/Loading Art.md`), instead of a random champion splash.
- Only the tips Zaviel picked from the wiki: Loading tips 2017 / Miscellaneous (3) and Loading tips 2025 / Default / Did You Know (149, by region), kept in `tools/data/loading-tips-pick.txt` in English. fetch_loading_tips.py finds each line in the game's English text (exact, else a close match) and takes Riot's zh_TW text of the same key; lines not found go to `Review/Loading Tip Keys.md`. The 2025 loading screen sorts tips into 你知道… and 祕訣 (game_startup_tip_category_1/2), but which tip is in which is not in the text file, hence the pick list.
- The Handbook's trivia card no longer shows champion lore or the 你知道嗎? label: it shows one of the game's own loading screen tips (zh_TW), centred on a dimmed random splash; random each visit, a tap shows another, none repeats until all were shown. Hidden while there are no tips.
- New `tools/fetch_loading_tips.py` (in update_patch.py): the tips from the game's string table (CommunityDragon lol.stringtable.json, as for the augment text) into `_data/loading-tips.json`; every other game_ key that looks like a tip is listed in `Review/Loading Tip Keys.md` to check nothing is missed. Taken: the loading screen's `game_startup_tip_<n>_<kind>` (背景故事 narrative, 遊戲技巧 gameplay, 冷知識 misc, 世界大賽 worlds, 遊戲禮儀 pb, the 2018 Garen/Darius lore) and the seasonal lore `game_startup_tip_narrative_y1s*/arc`; left out: April Fools cat facts, event modes (slime, plain-numbered), Arena (cherry), patch notices (game_tip_*). The wiki page the tips are listed on cannot be reached from the session.
