## ％ The %生命傷害 tag (Claude, for Zaviel)
- New ability tag %生命傷害 (damage that is a share of the target's health: max, current or missing), in the 傷害 group of the 篩選 panel, on the champion's tags and in the Handbook glossary: 75 champions, including 菲歐拉 (passive and R), 關 (passive), 法洛士 (W), 汎 (W) and 伊芙琳 (E).
- It is found in the game's own skill tooltips (a damage tag next to "最大 / 目前 / 已損失 生命"), because the short skill text does not say it (Fiora's does not). A tooltip rarely says whose health it is, so a skill that scales with the champion's OWN health (Sion's W is probably one) can be tagged too; one that says 自身 / 她的 is skipped.
- `tools/add_game_text.py` (was add_passive_rows.py) writes it into the existing notes; `fetch_data.py` does it for every champion. A sort by how big the percent is is not built.
- Not seen on a phone yet.
