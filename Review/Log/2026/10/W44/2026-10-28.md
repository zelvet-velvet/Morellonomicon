## 🃏 Tinder-style card swipe (Claude, for Zaviel)
- Handbook cards tilt as you swipe: a card leans left when it leaves to the left and leans right coming back, pivoting below the card.
- Pulling the last card: it tilts too, and the next topic grows out of the gap as a panel (icon, 下一篇, title) that fills the right side, turns accent-tinted when you pull far enough; let go and it opens, or the card springs back.
- Follow-up: the next-topic panel is now a fixed panel under the whole card (not a growing rectangle) that the card uncovers as it leaves; a tilted card's top now passes over the progress bars instead of being cut off.
- Not seen on a phone yet.
