## 🏷️ 14 ability tags in five groups (Claude, for Zaviel)
- New tags read from the skill text: 斬殺 (execute), 削弱 (blind, nearsight, grievous wounds, armour / magic resist / attack speed shred), 加速 (movement speed for self or allies), 隱身 (stealth / camouflage), 免傷 (damage reduction / untargetable) and 幫隊友 (heal, shield or speed that reaches allies). Re-read for all 173 champions (e.g. Graves 削弱 for his smoke, Katarina 加速, Leona 免傷).
- Groups: 傷害 (強化普攻 攻擊特效 斬殺), 控制 (控場 緩速 削弱), 移動 (位移 加速 隱身), 生存 (治療 護盾 吸血 免傷), 團隊 (幫隊友). The 篩選 panel and the legend show them grouped.
- A skill card names the kind of crowd control or weakening from its text, e.g. 控場 · 擊飛、暈眩 or 削弱 · 近視.
- Tag icons are the app's own again, all one style in their tag colour: the wiki's keyword and stat icons mix white, coloured and boxed pictures, and the phone will not recolour them (can return once stored in the vault). Role and lane icons stay the game's gold art. Pictures can no longer be picked up and dragged with a long press, and 全部清除 no longer moves the panel.
- Under the chart and on 速查 cards only the tags a champion has are shown (14 dashed chips would crowd them).
