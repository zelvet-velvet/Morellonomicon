- **Centred when short**: Home (the profile and the four ways in centred under the title), 最近對戰 with no games (a centred empty state), the augment page (an unrated augment no longer ends in blank space). Every empty state ("沒有符合", "還沒有技巧"…) now sits in the middle of its room.
