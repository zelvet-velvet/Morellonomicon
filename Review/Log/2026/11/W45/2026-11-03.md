## 🧪 Augment tiers rebuilt: reasoning checked against real games (Claude, for Zaviel)
- **New tiers for every champion**: each augment's S / A / B / C on the champion page now comes from why the champion's kit makes it work (dashes, hard crowd control, on-hit, crit, mana, healing allies, spins, channels…), checked against resg.top's Mayhem games for 16.21 (about 10 million games). The old fight simulation is gone.
- **The reason under an augment** says what in the kit makes it work (`2 個位移技能，位移冷卻大減`); when the games disagree, the augment's own effect line is shown instead.
- **No resg numbers in the vault**: the site has no licence, so its numbers stay in a local cache; only tiers and reasons are shown, the source named in [[Augment Model]].
- **Open questions** for Zaviel in [[Augment Questions]]: pairs strong in the games that no rule explains yet (`3ae6516`).
- **Zaviel's answers in**: Kai'Sa, Kog'Maw, Udyr, Shaco, Twisted Fate, Ashe and Bard get two tier lists (物攻 / 法術 or 攻速特效 / 法師) to switch between; Malphite's W + 裝甲衝擊, Qiyana's WQWQ, Hecarim's Q spam and more are explained on their pages (`ce19555`).
