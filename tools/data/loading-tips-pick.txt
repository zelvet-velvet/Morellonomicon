Bandle City
Time flows strangely in Bandle City, making yordles seem almost ageless to outsiders.
Some say yordles know secret ways through Runeterra that others cannot perceive.
Rumble's mech is named Tristy, in honor of a yordle who is very important to him.
Tristana's cannon is named Boomer.
Bilgewater
In Bilgewater, the dead are not always buried - they are often given back to the ocean in submerged caskets below bobbing tombstones.
Many structures in Bilgewater are made from remains of ships gutted upon its rocky shores.
When the wharf rats of Bilgewater gather in great swarms, they can devour an adult human in minutes.
The people of Bilgewater deeply respect the creatures of the deep, so much so, that their currency is Golden Krakens and Silver Serpents.
Bilgewater's boom-and-bust economy is based almost entirely on the hunting and harvesting of sea monsters.
The callers of the Serpent Isles sound enormous horns carved into the cliffs to beckon forth great sea monsters.
The Bearded Lady, and Mother Serpent, are both names for the Bilgewater deity Nagakabouros.
Miss Fortune's pistols were crafted by her mother for the young reaver Gangplank, who then turned the guns on their creator.
Illaoi wields "the Eye of God," an idol sacred to the Buhru.
Twisted Fate was born to the nomadic river-folk of the Serpentine, a river flowing near Bilgewater.
Tahm Kench is but one of the River King's many names. Some others may be better left unsaid.
Pyke made his name as a harpooner in Bloodharbor, a district of slaughter docks in Bilgewater.
Graves' shotgun is named Destiny.
Demacia
The Demacian capital, and seat of the monarchy, is the Great City of Demacia.

Native to northern Demacia are the rare, ferocious raptors. Only a few talented individuals are able to tame and ride these beasts.
Garen and Lux are members of the Crownguard family, which has protected the Demacian throne for generations.
Garen leads the Dauntless Vanguard, the kingdom of Demacia's most elite military force.
Lux trusted Sylas. Now Demacians are dead.
Fiora is the head of House Laurent in Demacia, a position she claimed upon killing her father in a duel.
Poppy was once close friends with a man named Orlon. She took up his hammer upon his death.
Galio was created by a legendary Demacian sculptor named Durand.
Quinn and her twin brother Caleb were born in the rural Demacian town of Uwendale.
Nocturne once haunted the Demacian city of Fossbarrow.
Freljord
Legends speak of Avarosa, a queen of the ancient Freljord, and how she was betrayed by her own sister.

Anivia, Ornn, Haestryr, and Volibear are among the countless demi-gods of the ancient Freljord.
The Frozen Watchers are believed to be trapped in the Howling Abyss.
According to legend, True Ice never melts. However, elemental magic or the power bestowed by demi-gods may test that truth…
Gnar was frozen in True Ice for a long, long time.

Nunu's yeti companion is named Willump. Willump's human companion is named Nunu.

Nunu's flute is named Svellsongur.
Brand spent his childhood in the outlying Freljordian settlement of Rygann's Reach.

Ionia
Ionian architecture is known for its harmonious fluidity with nature.

Yasuo and Yone both studied at the same school of swordcraft. It was this training that the brothers called upon in their fight to the death.
Shen became the Eye of Twilight after the death of his father, Great Master Kusho.
Zed was a member of the Kinkou, before he left and formed his Order of Shadow.
The myths of the Spirit Blossom festival serve as both tales of history and moral lessons.
The Gatekeeper. The Fox. The Khumaia. All are names for the same spirit celebrated during the Spirit Blossom festival.
There is a garden in southern Ionia where flowers consume memories.
Akali's favored weapons are the kama and kunai - a handheld sickle and a throwing dagger.
Irelia severed Swain's left arm during the Great Stand at Navori, halting the Noxian advance at the Placidium.
Xayah and Rakan are vastaya, from the Lhotlan tribelands in Ionia.
Rakan loves chocolate.
Kayn's master is Zed, who believes the young man could one day lead the Order of Shadow.
Kayn's full name is Shieda Kayn.
Khada Jhin is a stage name. Jhin's true identity remains a mystery.

Varus is a Darkin, reborn in the combined flesh of two Ionian hunters, Valmar and Kai.
Don't tell Sett's mom what he does for a living. We wouldn't want her to worry, right?

Rengar is one of the Kiilash, a vastayan tribe from Shurima.
Noxus
Gateways of dark stone known as Noxtoraa are raised over roads in territories conquered by Noxus.
Drakehounds, a distant relative of dragons, are kept by some rich, and perhaps foolish, Noxians as pets.
Warmasons are tasked by Noxus not only to plot and plan infrastructure, but also to scout out enemy territory for future invasions.
Annie lost her mother at a very young age. Her father, sister, and stepmother all died some years later.
The brothers Darius and Draven grew up as orphans in the port city of Basilich.

Piltover & Zaun
Piltovans call their home "the City of Progress." Zaunites typically use far less complimentary terms.
Progress Day is a celebration in Piltover, meant to commemorate the ingenuity of invention in their fair city. In Zaun, it is a day of remembrance.
Hextech is the fusion of magic and technology, harnessing the power contained within extremely rare crystals.
Thanks to her hextech heart, Camille has managed to retain some of her youthful looks despite her age.
Caitlyn's rifle is unique among the Enforcers, outfitted with the very best of hextech. Being childhood friends with Jayce Talis has its advantages.
Jayce's transforming hammer is powered by hextech, and owes its design to Jayce's hours toiling in the forges of his family's business.
Vi wields a pair of gauntlets imbued with hextech power. They were crafted by Jayce Talis, their original purpose to handle superheated objects.
Ezreal discovered his gauntlet inside the lost tomb of Ne'Zuk, in Shurima.
Like the ancient Shurimans that once inhabited their land, modern-day Zaunites revere Janna— or Jan'ahrem— as a goddess, said to protect her followers from furious tempests.
Though the undercity of Zaun falls under the jurisdiction of the Enforcers, the chembarons and their gangs are not particularly interested in being ruled by distant topsiders.
Zaun's wealthiest can often be found on the city's Promenade levels, which intersect with Piltover's lowest regions.
The Entresol levels of Zaun are where most of the city's trade and business occur.
The Sump is the lowest region of Zaun. Few choose to dwell here for long.
Years of polluting the atmosphere with chemical fumes caused a near-permanent blanket of noxious vapor to fester in Zaun. At its lowest levels, "the Gray" can be, at times, fatal to breathe.
Blitzcrank was created by Viktor, and eventually became stronger, faster, and smarter than any golem or automaton.
Ekko is the leader of the Firelights, a band of Zaunites known for their fiery devotion to building a better future… often at the expense of Piltover property.
Jinx's rocket launcher, mini-gun, and electric pistol are named Fishbones, Pow-Pow, and Zapper.
Dr. Mundo isn't actually a doctor. He just thinks he is.
A former Noxian headsman, Urgot was betrayed and imprisoned in the Dredge, a mine deep beneath Zaun.

Shadow Isles
King Viego of Camavor fell in love with a seamstress, Isolde, a thousand years ago - beginning a fairytale romance that would lead to ruin.
Viego hails from the distant land of Camavor. In his thousand-year absence, his kingdom has fallen, forgotten.
The grieving king Viego tried to bring his dead queen back to life in the magical waters of the Blessed Isles. He failed, giving rise to the Ruination.
Before the Ruination, the Shadow Isles were known as the Blessed Isles.
The Blessed Isles were home to a society of scholars, who gathered magical artifacts and historical records from across Runeterra.
The wraith Kalista once faithfully served the Ruined King, Viego. This loyalty cost Kalista her life.
In life, Hecarim led the Iron Order, an infamous brotherhood of mounted knights.
Using enchanted waters, Maokai turned the desolate islands of his birth into a verdant paradise.
Thresh's lantern is a prison for countless souls, including that of Senna, Lucian's wife—who later escaped…
Yorick wears the Tears of Life, a vial of water drawn from the Blessed Isles' sacred springs.
Ordinary citizens have no idea that the Sentinels of Light patrol Runeterra with ancient relic weapons, keeping them safe from the Shadow Isles' wraiths.
Shurima
Deep beneath Shurima's ancient capital lies the Oasis of the Dawn, believed by many to be the source of all rivers in the land.
Some Shuriman nomads have created entire towns upon the backs of the dormun - enormous beasts that wander the desert.
The northern coast of Shurima is partially controlled by Noxus, though the rebirth of Shurima's Emperor Azir may bring that to an end.
Those Ascended by the Sun Disc of Shurima can live for thousands of years.
Nasus and Renekton were mortal brothers, and joined the ranks of the Ascended Host together.
In Shurima, those who survived a failed Ascension ritual are known as the Baccai, who are often twisted and malformed.
The monstrous Darkin were once noble warriors of the Shuriman Empire.
Deep in Shurima, the sand itself flows like water. Perhaps it is this constant shifting that keeps Brand, the vengeful fire mage, trapped beneath its dunes.

Sivir is a descendent of Azir, the Ascended god-emperor of Shurima.

Zilean is a survivor of Icathia's destruction by the Void.
