# Handbook decks (Morellonomicon)

Before a change, grep the Handbook card section of `Dev Tools/Layouts.md` (do not read the whole note: its `mn-layout` JSON is long).

- **Handbook decks.** Every card follows the Handbook card section of [[Layouts]] (frame, dock, text limits, stage templates); `python3 -I tools/check_cards.py` lists the cards over the limits. A guide note (`新手入門/`) is a swipeable deck: each `## ` heading is one card; under it `icon: <lucide>` and `visual: <kind> <args>` lines, then one or two short lines (no paragraphs, more picture than text). Visual kinds: `video Ahri Q`, `img <vault path or https>`, `style Ahri` (the 戰鬥風格 chart), `facts Ahri` (the 技能傷害 block), `tags Ahri`, `roles`, `lanes`, `tabs` (the champion page's four tabs), `damage` (the three damage kinds), `map [habackground|arambilgewater|arambloom]` (an ARAM loading screen picture; no name = random), `splash Swain` (a champion's splash art), `skill Gwen Q` (one ability large with Riot's text), `tree 3071` (an item's recipe drawn like the shop; use an item whose parts differ, since a part listed twice shows once), `counters [刺客 …]` (role icons with coloured arrows for who counters whom; named roles light up), `wards lane|dragon|baron|steal` (the Summoner's Rift minimap with ward icons, a sketch of where vision helps), `ability Malphite R` / `clip Pyke R 0-4.5` (or `clip Jayce Q2`, the second clip; `a-b` plays only those seconds; `a-b+c-d` plays those pieces in turn and skips what lies between, e.g. a fast-forward) / `levels Malphite R [300]` (per rank, the damage alone and with 300 of the stat it scales with) / `forms Jayce 戰鎚 *加農` / `formset Jayce 戰鎚 加農` (both forms' ability pictures, from CommunityDragon's `<id><key>_melee|ranged.png`) (layout templates from `plugin/src/pages/cardkit.ts`: components such as the skill head, tag pills, numbers per rank with scaling, stat boxes and form columns are placed in named grid areas, so every card of a kind has the same structure; add a template there), `skills Ahri`, `role 戰士`, `champs Ahri Garen`, `itemtag Armor`, `item 3089`, `augs 稜鏡`, `radar Ahri` (see `plugin/src/pages/deck.ts`). Pulling the deck's last card further left reveals the next topic and opens it when pulled far enough. The picture fills the card (video and `img` edge to edge), so pick visuals that read at that size; for a role use iconic champions (`champs`), not `role` (a daily random six). Frontmatter: `level` (`basic` = 初來乍到, `advanced` = 高手必備, the two blocks on the Handbook home), `order`, `icon`, `summary`, `cover` (the topic's picture on the Handbook home: a champion id for its splash, or `map habackground|arambilgewater|arambloom`); `layout: list` shows a note as a plain list (the glossary). Write the text with full-width punctuation (，。：（）), so lines break between phrases; a closing `**小技巧：** …` (any short bold label with a colon) becomes the card's tip box. Use Riot's official zh_TW terms (增幅裝置, 銀級 / 金級 / 稜鏡級, 隨機單中:大混戰). Keep colours muted; no high-saturation fills.
