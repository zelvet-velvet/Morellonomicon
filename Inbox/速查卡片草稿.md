Drafts for the 速查 cards (layout v4: 概覽 · 玩法 · 技能 · 風格), written by Claude from memory of how each champion plays, not from a data source, so **check every line**. Edit in place; nothing reads this note yet. Once it is right, a script copies it into each champion note (`punch`, `stack`, `soul`, `focus`).

- **一句話** (`punch`, tab 1): what the champion is, in keywords, ≤ 14 字.
- **堆** (`stack`, tab 2 chips): what to build towards, two or three from this list only: 法術強度 攻擊力 攻速 技能急速 暴擊 生命 護甲 魔防 穿透 跑速.
- **玩法** (`soul`, tab 2): how to abuse the champion, two or three lines, ≤ 16 字 each, one per `-` line.
- **主力** (`focus`, optional): only for a champion whose one ability is the point (stacking, a quest, the hook). Most have none: leave it empty.
- `(待補)`: I did not know the champion well enough. The eight champions you wrote yourself are at the end, with your text kept; only 堆 and 主力 are new there.
